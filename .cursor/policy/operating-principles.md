# Operating Principles

1. Repository truth over conversation history.
2. Minimum sufficient context.
3. Explicit ownership and authority.
4. Separate planning, implementation, validation, and review.
5. Evidence over claims.
6. No silent scope expansion.
7. Protected decisions require the proper authority.
8. Prefer the smallest sufficient change.
9. Durable decisions must be persisted.
10. New evidence may change the plan, but not silently change the goal.
11. Work is routed through explicit workflows.
12. Agents receive context for their responsibility, not the whole project.
13. Process depth must match risk; small local work should stay lightweight.
